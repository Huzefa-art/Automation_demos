# Walkthrough: Real Estate Lead Management Automation

I have successfully prepared all the components for your Real Estate Lead Management automation. Everything is designed to work within the free tiers of Make.com, HubSpot, and Google.

## What's Included

### 1. [SETUP.md](file:///home/huzefa/.gemini/antigravity/brain/d08e75a9-88ff-4c1a-abe0-b68099d1ec15/SETUP.md)
Comprehensive, step-by-step instructions for non-technical users. Covers account creation, service connection, and scenario import.

### 2. [scenario.json](file:///home/huzefa/.gemini/antigravity/brain/d08e75a9-88ff-4c1a-abe0-b68099d1ec15/scenario.json)
The complete Make.com blueprint. You can import this directly to see the following flow:
- **Trigger**: New Facebook Lead.
- **Search**: Checks Google Sheets for duplicate emails.
- **Filter**: Only proceeds if the lead is new.
- **Action**: Adds the lead to Google Sheets.
- **Action**: Syncs the lead to HubSpot CRM.
- **Action**: Sends a personalized confirmation email to the lead.
- **Action**: Sends a notification alert to your sales agent.

### 3. [templates.md](file:///home/huzefa/.gemini/antigravity/brain/d08e75a9-88ff-4c1a-abe0-b68099d1ec15/templates.md)
Contains the exact Google Sheet column headers and the professionally written email templates for both the lead and the agent.

### 4. [testing_guide.md](file:///home/huzefa/.gemini/antigravity/brain/d08e75a9-88ff-4c1a-abe0-b68099d1ec15/testing_guide.md)
A guide on how to use the Facebook Lead Ads Testing Tool to simulate a lead and verify every step of the automation without spending a dime.

## Key Features
- **Zero Cost**: Built entirely on free tiers.
- **Duplicate Prevention**: Automatically checks if a lead already exists before adding them.
- **Multi-Channel**: Syncs data to Sheets and CRM while handling two-way notifications.
- **Plug-and-Play**: The JSON file maps the fields automatically once connections are established.
